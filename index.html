FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about dental health, treatments, and what to expect at Smile Dental Hospital.


💬 Ask Us Directly
How To Improve Your Smile? +
People who want to improve their smile usually want teeth that are whiter, straighter, and don't have dark or discolored fillings. Modern dental care has made even complete smile makeovers much more gentle and easier than ever before. Options range from simple whitening to full cosmetic makeovers.
What Are Cavities / Tooth Decay? +
Cavities are permanently damaged areas in or around the surface of your tooth that result in tiny holes. They are caused by plaque from food high in sugar or starch settling on the surface, where bacteria feed and cause the protective enamel to slowly deteriorate. According to WHO, about 60–90% of kids worldwide have cavities.
What Is Gum Disease? +
Gum disease is one of the most common dental diseases adults get, with three stages starting from reversible gingivitis to serious periodontal disease. WHO states 15–25% of adults aged 35–44 worldwide have severe periodontal disease. Early treatment is critical to prevent tooth loss.
Why Do Teeth Get Sensitive? +
Sensitivity happens for several reasons including tooth decay, fractured teeth, worn fillings, gum disease, worn tooth enamel, or exposed tooth roots. Your dentist may recommend desensitizing toothpaste or an alternative treatment based on the root cause. Proper oral hygiene is key to prevention.
What Is Oral Cancer? +
Oral cancer is one of the most serious dental problems. Most begin as a small, pale, pinkish lump or growth inside the mouth — almost always painless at first. Regular dental check-ups are vital for early detection, especially for smokers and those who drink excessively. Early detection dramatically improves survival rates.
What Are Dental Emergencies? +
Common emergencies include a broken or cracked tooth, an abscessed tooth, or a tooth knocked out in an accident. Call us immediately at 996 3030 390 for emergency dental care. While many toothaches can be avoided with regular visits, accidents can and do happen — we are here 24/7 for emergencies.
